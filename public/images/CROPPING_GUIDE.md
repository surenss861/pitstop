# Cropping guide — final assets before swap

Use this when preparing each photo before you replace the placeholders in `public/images/`. Same filenames; just crop, blur plates/addresses, then export.

**Before any crop:** blur or remove license plates, house numbers, and identifiable addresses.

---

## Hero (`hero/`)

### `hero-bentley.jpg`
- **Shot:** Black Bentley exterior, front 3/4, glossy.
- **Aspect:** **Landscape 16:9 or 3:2** (e.g. 1200×675 or 1200×800).
- **Crop:** Car fills ~60–70% of frame. Trim excess driveway/sky; keep reflections. Slight margin above/below for different viewport heights.
- **Min width:** 1200px for sharp hero on desktop.

### `hero-mercedes.jpg`
- **Shot:** Silver Mercedes coupe in driveway.
- **Aspect:** **Landscape 16:9 or 3:2**.
- **Crop:** Same as above — car as subject, minimal house/background. Works as Toronto/local hero so keep it clean and “at your location” without distracting detail.
- **Min width:** 1200px.

### `exterior-mercedes.jpg`

## Gallery — interior

### `interior-tesla-black.jpg`
- **Landscape 4:3 or 16:9.** Dash/seats/steering — one clear focal area. Crop out door frames if messy; keep it “clean interior result.”

### `interior-tesla-white.jpg`
- **Landscape 4:3 or 16:9.** Same idea; bright, clean, readable at thumbnail size.

### `interior-defender-front.jpg`
- **Landscape 4:3.** Driver area / front cabin. Tighter crop than full cabin if it strengthens the shot.

### `interior-alfa-red.jpg`

**Interior rule:** **Landscape** so they sit well in grid. Min width ~800px. Avoid wide empty footwell; center the “result.”

## Export settings

- **Format:** JPG.
- **Quality:** 80–85% for gallery; 85–90% for hero (balance size vs sharpness).
- **sRGB** for web.
- **Min widths:** Hero 1200px; gallery 800px (or 1000px if you want extra sharpness on retina).

After you replace the files, check homepage, gallery, and Toronto on desktop and mobile to confirm nothing is stretched or awkward.
